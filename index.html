About Me

A Little About Myself

I'm from Ukraine. I came to England for Year 10, to Queen Ethelburga's Collegiate in York — new country, new school, every lesson in my third language.

A few months into that first year, the war started. Home stopped being somewhere I could simply go back to; getting to school meant leaving without knowing when — or whether — I would see it again. I sat my GCSEs checking the news from home between exams. You learn to work with part of your mind permanently somewhere else. You also learn, very fast, how to adapt and keep going anyway.

I kept going — and in Year 11, in the middle of all of it, I was awarded a full scholarship: a full ride. A second academic scholarship followed for sixth form. I finished with A*AA at A Level and an A* EPQ, served as a school prefect, and came out of it certain of one thing: I wanted to build things. I'm now in my second year of BSc Computer Science with Management at Queen Mary University of London, after a first year averaging 72% (First Class). Since then I've shipped a bilingual marketing site and a members' web app for a wellness club, built Java and Python systems at university, and run brand design for a Ukrainian cosmetics company still trading under wartime conditions.

Before any of it, I was a professional ballroom dancer, competing across Europe and China. I still take masterclasses in London. Different stage, same discipline.
